Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Application Purpose

AR ERP is an internal business application used to manage authorized eBay seller accounts.

Information We Access

With seller authorization, the application may access eBay account information, listings, inventory, orders, feedback, marketing analytics, performance data, and financial information through official eBay APIs.

Use of Information

Information is used only for internal business operations, including inventory management, order processing, advertising analysis, financial reconciliation, and operational reporting.

Data Sharing

We do not sell eBay user information and do not share it with unrelated third parties.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect authentication credentials and eBay account data.

Data Retention

Data is retained only as necessary for legitimate business and compliance purposes.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the application administrator.
